# Kaigo Module Illustration Prompts

Prompt gambar profesional (teks saja) untuk ilustrasi modul Kaigo. Gunakan
dengan alat image-gen pilihan Anda. Gaya konsisten: bersih, medis-edukatif,
menghormati martabat lansia, tanpa teks di dalam gambar.

**Gaya dasar (tempelkan ke setiap prompt):**
`clean flat illustration, soft muted palette (washi cream, matcha green, warm brown), respectful and dignified tone, Japanese care setting, no text, educational diagram style, gentle lighting`

---

## Praktik Perawatan

**車椅子介助 (Wheelchair)** — A care worker gently pushing an elderly person in a wheelchair up a small ramp, worker's hands on the grips, brakes visible, calm expression, nursing-home corridor background.

**歩行介助 (Walking assistance)** — A caregiver standing slightly behind and to the weak side of an elderly person using a cane, one hand supporting the back, handrail along the wall, safe posture.

**移乗 (Transfer)** — A care worker demonstrating correct body mechanics while transferring an elderly person from bed to wheelchair, knees bent, back straight, wheelchair brakes locked, footrests up.

**入浴介助 (Bath assistance)** — A caregiver assisting an elderly person at a shower chair in an accessible bathroom, privacy respected (towel draped), warm atmosphere, non-slip mat.

**食事介助 (Meal assistance)** — A caregiver seated beside an elderly person at eye level, offering a spoon, the person upright in a chair with chin slightly tucked, pleasant dining setting.

**排泄介助 (Toilet assistance)** — Dignity-focused scene of an accessible toilet with grab bars and a caregiver's supportive hand on a handrail, privacy curtain, no exposure, respectful framing.

---

## Kesehatan & Medis

**バイタルサイン (Vital signs)** — Close-up of a caregiver measuring blood pressure on an elderly person's arm with a cuff, a pulse oximeter on the fingertip, calm clinical setting.

**服薬支援 (Medication support)** — A caregiver checking a labeled medication organizer against a chart, glass of water ready, careful "5 rights" verification, clean table.

**心肺蘇生 (CPR)** — Instructional diagram of chest compression hand placement at the center of the chest on a training manikin, arrows showing depth and rhythm, first-aider kneeling.

**認知症ケア (Dementia care)** — A caregiver making gentle eye contact at the front of a seated elderly person with dementia, warm reassuring expression, calm room, person-centered care.

---

## Keselamatan & Darurat

**火災避難 (Fire evacuation)** — A caregiver guiding elderly residents toward an illuminated emergency exit, low posture under light smoke, calm orderly evacuation, exit sign glowing.

**地震・災害対応 (Earthquake response)** — A caregiver protecting an elderly person's head with a cushion during shaking, sheltering beside a sturdy table, emergency kit visible nearby.

---

## Bahasa & Komunikasi

**面接対策 (Interview)** — A neatly dressed foreign care candidate bowing respectfully at a job interview, interviewer at a desk, calm professional office, warm encouraging mood.

**接遇マナー (Hospitality)** — A caregiver greeting an elderly person with a warm bow and gentle smile at a nursing-home entrance, tidy uniform, welcoming atmosphere.

**職場文化 (Workplace culture)** — A multinational care team in a morning huddle (報連相), sharing information around a whiteboard, collaborative and respectful mood.

---

## Etika & Hukum

**守秘義務 (Confidentiality)** — A caregiver placing a resident's record file into a locked cabinet, discreet setting, symbolizing privacy and information security, no readable text.

**尊厳と自立 (Dignity & independence)** — A caregiver supporting—not doing for—an elderly person buttoning their own shirt, encouraging expression, emphasizing residual ability.
